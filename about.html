About

Francis Chinonso Ikedinobi, known online as OfficialXandy.

OfficialXandy is the online name of Francis Chinonso Ikedinobi, an independent game developer, software engineer and digital creator from Calabar, Cross River State, Nigeria.

He is the creator of UNICAL Life 3D, a 3D browser game set at the University of Calabar, where he is also a chemistry student.

Alongside games and software, he creates XandyCore, a channel of AI-animated retellings of rare African legends told episode by episode, launched in October 2026. He also makes Naija comedy shorts and video edits.
