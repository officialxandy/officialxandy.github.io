About

Francis Chinonso Ikedinobi, known online as OfficialXandy.

An independent game developer, software engineer and digital creator from Calabar, Cross River State, Nigeria, and a chemistry student at the University of Calabar.

He builds UNICAL Life 3D, a browser game set at the University of Calabar, and creates XandyCore, a channel of AI-animated retellings of rare African legends.
